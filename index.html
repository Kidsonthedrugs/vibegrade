How this works

No black box. Every number below is reproducible from the chain.

The curve

vibe/vibe V6 launches are constant-product curves on virtual reserves. The constants are fixed by the protocol, not by the creator:

VT0 = 800M × 4/3        virtual token reserve
V0  = target / 3        virtual pair reserve (target = 4 ETH on ETH pairs)
k   = VT0 × V0

Buying

netIn     = amountIn × 10000 / (10000 + taxBps)   ← fee on top
tokensOut = (VT0 − sold) − k / (V0 + pairPrincipal + netIn)
price     = (V0 + pairPrincipal) / (VT0 − sold)

Selling

proceeds = (V0 + pairPrincipal) × tokens / ((VT0 − sold) + tokens)
net      = proceeds − proceeds × taxBps / 10000    ← fee off proceeds

Why graduation has no price gap

When pairPrincipal = target, solving the equation gives sold = 800,000,000 exactly, and the curve price equals the Uniswap v4 pool's opening price (2e-8 ETH/token). The token does not jump at the moment it graduates.

Verified, not assumed

These formulas were checked against the contract's own quoteBuyExactIn and quoteSell: token amounts match to a relative error of ~1e-16, sells to 1 wei of integer rounding. The test suite runs on captured on-chain quotes.

Launch score

Six components, weighted and published: tax fit (20), weight balance (25), graduation airdrop (20), pair choice (10), opening buy (15), distribution (10). It is advice about configuration quality — not a prediction of price or returns.
